📄 Automatic CV Generator

What is a CV? Your 1-page professional story. Employers decide in 6 seconds if they call you. A good CV = Name, Goal, Education, Skills, Experience, Referee. We will guide you step-by-step - one question at a time.

Step 1/6: Your Name & Contacts

Write your full official name as on ID.

Step 2/6: Your Course

What are you studying? Employers want to know.

Step 3/6: Career Goal

In 1-2 lines, what job do you want? Example: "To use my teaching skills to inspire learners..."

Step 4/6: Skills

List 4-6 skills. Example: Communication, Computer, Teamwork, Time Management

Step 5/6: Experience

Even attachment, church, volunteer counts! Example: "2025 - Teaching Practice, Eldoret Primary"

Step 6/6: Referee

Someone who can confirm you are good. Example: Lecturer, Pastor, Former boss.
